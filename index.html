How we care

Rescue

From beach to safety.

Injured and vulnerable seals are assessed and brought into specialist care.

Care

Everything they need.

Treatment, nutrition and dedicated care help them begin their journey.

Rehabilitate

Getting stronger.

Seals build strength and independence while human contact is kept limited.

Release

Back where they belong.

When they’re ready, they’re returned to the wild.

Found a seal on
the beach?

Don’t approach it.

Seals regularly rest on land and don’t always need rescuing.

Keep your distance

Keep people and dogs well away.

Observe

Look for obvious wounds, entanglement, difficulty breathing or other signs of distress.

Report your concern

If you see a seal in distress or looking injured, please call BDMLR.

Call BDMLR 01825 765 546

For expert advice, call British Divers Marine Life Rescue (BDMLR).
