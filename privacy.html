Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Tynemouth Seal Hospital (registered charity 1197677) uses personal information through this website. We are the controller of information you provide to us through enquiries. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email tynemouthsh@gmail.com.

Information we receive

You can read the website without creating an account. If you contact us, we receive your name, email address, enquiry category and message, plus any phone number or organisation you choose to provide. We also receive information you include in later correspondence. Please do not send payment details, health information or other sensitive personal information through the form.

The form uses Formspree to transmit and store submissions and deliver them to our email inbox. Its service may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the service and prevent abuse. GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Fonts and website images are hosted with the site; we have not added analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use your information

We use enquiry information to answer questions, arrange requested follow-up and manage volunteering, fundraising, donations or other support you ask about. Our lawful basis for ordinary enquiries is our legitimate interest in responding to people and running the charity. We consider your privacy and use only information needed for that purpose. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

If an enquiry leads to a separate arrangement, such as volunteering, we will explain any additional information needed and how it will be used. We may keep or disclose relevant records when necessary to comply with a legal obligation or deal with legal claims. We do not use the contact form for automated decisions or profiling.

Who receives information

Enquiries are available to authorised people handling them for the hospital, our form provider Formspree and our email service provider. Hosting and service providers also process technical information needed to operate and protect their services. We do not sell enquiry information. We may share information with professional advisers or public authorities where necessary and lawful.

Read Formspree’s privacy information, GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy for their handling of service data.

Optional Facebook and Google Maps content

Facebook and Google Maps are blocked until you choose to load them. Loading an embed connects your browser to Meta or Google and may share your IP address, browser information and the page you are viewing. The provider may use cookies, recognise an existing account or process information for its own purposes, including personalisation and advertising.

Your choice to load an embed is optional. Use its “Turn off” button to stop it loading on the current page, or leave the page. The choice is not saved for later visits. Turning it off cannot undo information already received or delete cookies belonging to another provider. See our Cookies page for details and provider controls.

Donations, shopping and external websites

Payments are taken on JustGiving, and shopping takes place on Amazon or Teemill, rather than on this website. We do not collect card details here. Those services provide their own privacy information. They may share donation or order information with the charity according to their terms and your choices; any subsequent charity records are outside the contact form itself.

How long information is kept

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and any related follow-up. When deciding whether a record is still needed, we consider whether the matter remains open, whether an ongoing volunteering or fundraising arrangement needs it, and whether a legal obligation or dispute requires its retention. Records no longer needed should be deleted from both Formspree and the email inbox, subject to provider backup arrangements. You can contact us to ask about retention of your particular enquiry.

Processing outside the UK

Our providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside the UK, including in the United States. Different rules may apply in those countries. The relevant provider notices explain their international processing. Contact us for details of the safeguards applicable to your information and how to obtain a copy. Our use of a provider does not mean that your information stays in the UK.

Your choices and rights

Providing enquiry information is voluntary, but we need a name, reply address, category and message to handle the form. Without sufficient details we may be unable to respond. Phone number and organisation are optional.

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or erase your information, restrict its use, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or receive a portable copy where that right applies. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights are not absolute; we will explain any applicable limits. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

Email tynemouthsh@gmail.com to make a request or raise a concern. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when the website or the way we handle information changes. The review date appears above.
