Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies include browser storage and other ways of reading or saving information on a device. This page explains what the website uses and the choices available to you.

When you browse the site

Our website code does not set cookies or use local storage, session storage, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are hosted locally. Your browser may cache files to load pages efficiently. Hosting security logs are separate from cookies; see our Privacy Notice.

Optional external content

Facebook feeds and Google Maps do not load automatically. You can read the site, contact us and use direct links without loading either embed. Each load button applies only to that provider on the current page; we do not save your choice between visits.

Facebook — Meta

If you choose to load Facebook, Meta may store or read cookies for its service, security, measurement, personalisation and advertising. What it uses can depend on your Facebook login, browser and provider settings. Read Meta’s cookie policy for its current cookie descriptions and durations and Meta’s privacy policy for its use of personal information.

Google Maps — Google

If you choose to load the map, Google may use cookies and similar technologies to provide and secure its services, remember preferences, measure usage and personalise content or advertising, depending on your settings. Read Google’s cookie information for current types and durations and Google’s privacy policy.

Changing your choice

To decline an embed, leave its load button untouched. After loading it, select “Turn off Facebook feed” or “Turn off Google map” beside it. This removes the embedded content and stops further requests from it on that page. Reloading or leaving the page also resets the choice.

Turning an embed off does not delete third-party cookies already stored or withdraw information already sent. Use your browser’s cookie and site-data settings to remove or block those cookies, and the provider’s privacy controls to manage its further use of information. Blocking third-party cookies may prevent embeds from working, but the direct links remain available.

Forms and other services

Formspree is contacted when you submit an enquiry. It may apply service security and anti-spam measures; its privacy policy explains its processing. We do not load a Formspree tracking script on page views.

Following a link to JustGiving, Amazon, Teemill, Facebook, Instagram or another website takes you to a separate service. That service manages its own cookies and consent choices. Our embed choices do not apply to those websites.

Questions

Email tynemouthsh@gmail.com with questions about this website. We will update this page if we introduce different cookies or services.
